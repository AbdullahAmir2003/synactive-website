Security Awareness and Training

Deliver practical, organization specific security awareness training, then assess employee understanding with a scoped phishing campaign.

1 month

Deliverables

  • Training slides
  • PDF guidebook
  • Phishing campaign report with click and data submission statistics, plus recommendations
  • Comprehensive project plan before execution

How it works

Synactive first learns the organization’s business requirements and relevant risks. Training materials and a brief knowledge check follow. The agreed phishing campaign is then designed and run, and results are presented in the final week.

Week 1Understand the organization’s requirements, risks, and threats.
Week 2Deliver training slides, PDF resources, and a short knowledge check.
Week 3Design and run the campaign based on the agreed scenario.
Week 4Present the campaign report and recommendations.

The organization covers the campaign domain cost. User level interaction reporting can be discussed during scoping.
