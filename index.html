1. Red Teaming

7 services

Security Awareness and Training

Build practical employee awareness with training tailored to your organization, supporting materials, and an authorized phishing campaign to measure understanding.

1 month

Deliverables

  • Training slides and PDF guidebook
  • Phishing campaign report with interaction statistics and recommendations
  • Project plan before execution

Engagement outline

The engagement begins with a review of business requirements and risks, followed by training, a short knowledge check, the agreed phishing campaign, and a presentation of results and recommendations.

Week 1Understand the organization's business requirements, risks, and threats.
Week 2Deliver training slides, PDF resources, and a short knowledge check.
Week 3Design and run the phishing campaign based on the agreed scenario.
Week 4Share the campaign report, interaction statistics, and recommendations.

The organization is responsible for the campaign domain cost. Reporting on user interactions can be discussed during scoping.

External Recon Assessment

Map publicly accessible services and identify opportunities to reduce your digital footprint and external attack surface.

1 month

Deliverables

  • External Recon Assessment Report with identified exposures, business impact, and recommendations
  • Project plan before execution

Engagement outline

The engagement begins with a kickoff and information request, followed by passive and active reconnaissance of agreed assets. Findings and recommendations are presented at project closure.

Week 1Kickoff, business context, and information request.
Week 2Passive review of the public footprint and available exposure information.
Week 3Active reconnaissance of agreed public assets.
Week 4Present findings and recommendations.

This assessment does not include exploitation.

External Red Team Assessment

A black box assessment that simulates an external attacker and shows the potential business impact of attacks against assets exposed to the internet.

1 to 3 months

Deliverables

  • External Red Team Assessment Report with findings, evidence, business impact, and recommendations
  • Project plan before execution

Engagement outline

The engagement begins with a kickoff and information request. Testing scope and duration depend on the agreed assets and requirements; findings are presented at project closure.

Assumed Breach Assessment

A grey box assessment that starts from an agreed position with limited access and examines how an attacker could gain more access within the environment.

1 to 3 months

Deliverables

  • Assumed Breach Assessment Report with findings, supporting evidence, business impact, and recommendations
  • Project plan before execution

Engagement outline

The kickoff and information request establish the rules and starting conditions. Scope determines duration; the report is shared at project closure.

Internal Red Team Assessment

A white box assessment of agreed internal assets, using the system information you provide to identify weaknesses and misconfigurations.

2 to 4 months

Deliverables

  • Internal Red Team Assessment Report with findings, evidence, business impact, and recommendations
  • Project plan before execution

Engagement outline

The kickoff and information request define the assets and requirements included in the assessment. Duration depends on scope; reporting follows project closure.

Web Application Penetration Test

Assess the web applications included in the agreed scope using a black box, grey box, or white box approach selected with your organization.

1 to 2 months

Deliverables

  • Web Application Penetration Test Report with findings, evidence, business impact, and recommendations
  • Project plan before execution

Approach

Testing follows the OWASP Top 10. Scope and duration are confirmed during kickoff.

A staging or test environment is recommended.

AI Red Team Assessment

Evaluate AI systems included in the agreed scope for security and safety failures, including prompt injection, jailbreaks, sensitive data exposure, and excessive agency.

1 to 2 months

Deliverables

  • AI Red Team Assessment Report with findings, evidence, business impact, and recommendations
  • Project plan before execution

Approach

We agree on a black box, grey box, or white box testing approach. The assessment references OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications and MITRE ATLAS.

A staging or test environment is recommended to avoid disrupting production systems. Duration depends on scope.

2. Governance, Risk & Compliance

7 services

ISO/IEC 27001 Implementation

Build an Information Security Management System (ISMS) from the ground up, aligning policies, risk assessment, and security controls with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

3 to 6 months

Deliverables

  • Gap Assessment Report
  • Asset and Risk Registers and a Risk Treatment Plan
  • Statement of Applicability and core security policies

Engagement outline

The kickoff and information request establish your context. Deliverables are prepared in phases and finalized at project closure to support external certification readiness.

A project plan is shared before work begins. Timing depends on agreed scope and requirements.

ISO/IEC 27001 Internal Audit

Independently evaluate your existing ISMS against ISO/IEC 27001:2022, identify nonconformities, and review control effectiveness before external certification.

1 month

Deliverables

  • Internal Audit Report covering conformities, nonconformities, observations, and corrective action recommendations

Engagement outline

The kickoff and information request establish the audit scope. The audit reviews Clauses 4 to 10 of the management system and applicable Annex A controls, then concludes with the audit report.

Week 1Kickoff and information request.
Week 2Review Clauses 4 to 10 of the management system.
Week 3Review applicable Annex A controls.
Week 4Deliver the audit report.

A project plan is shared before work begins. Timing depends on agreed scope and requirements.

Establish a Privacy Information Management System (PIMS) to govern personally identifiable information managed by PII controllers and processors. The system can operate independently or alongside ISO/IEC 27001.

3 to 6 months

Deliverables

  • Privacy Gap Assessment and Record of Processing Activities
  • Privacy Risk Register and Privacy Statement of Applicability
  • Privacy policies

Engagement outline

The kickoff and information request establish project scope. Deliverables are shared in phases and finalized at project closure.

A project plan is shared before work begins. Timing depends on agreed scope and requirements.

ISO/IEC 27701 Internal Audit

Independently review PIMS governance and privacy controls against ISO/IEC 27701 requirements to support readiness for independent certification.

1 month

Deliverables

  • Internal Audit Report covering privacy conformities, nonconformities, observations, and remediation recommendations

Engagement outline

The kickoff and information request establish the audit scope. Review Clauses 4 to 10 of the PIMS and applicable controls for PII controllers and processors, then deliver the audit report.

Week 1Kickoff and information request.
Week 2Review Clauses 4 to 10 of the PIMS.
Week 3Review applicable controls for PII controllers and processors.
Week 4Deliver the audit report.

A project plan is shared before work begins. Timing depends on agreed scope and requirements.
