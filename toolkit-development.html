Toolkit Development

Create compliance documentation tailored to your objectives for one, two, or all three frameworks: ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27701, and ISO/IEC 42001.

1 month

Deliverables

  • Customized management system Clauses 4 through 10
  • Operational risk, asset, and Statement of Applicability registers
  • Supporting control documents for selected frameworks

How it works

Documentation is prepared over four weeks after kickoff and an information request.

Week 1Kickoff and information request.
Week 2Draft management system Clauses 4 through 10.
Week 3Draft operational registers and Statements of Applicability.
Week 4Prepare supporting documents for applicable controls.
