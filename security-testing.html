1. Red Teaming7 services

Understand what an attacker could actually reach.

Authorized, scoped testing across your public footprint, workforce, applications, AI systems, and internal environment, followed by clear findings and recommendations.

Discuss a Red Team engagement
Assessments and training

Seven ways to examine exposure.

Every engagement is scoped with your organization. The timelines below are indicative ranges from the service definition and may depend on project scope.

Security Awareness and Training

Build practical employee awareness with organization specific training, supporting materials, and an authorized phishing campaign to evaluate understanding.

1 month

Deliverables

  • Training slides and PDF guidebook
  • Phishing campaign report with interaction statistics and recommendations
  • Project plan before execution

Engagement outline

Understand business requirements and risks, deliver training and a short knowledge check, run the agreed phishing campaign, then present results and recommendations.

Week 1Understand the organization's business requirements, risks, and threats.
Week 2Deliver training slides, PDF resources, and a short knowledge check.
Week 3Design and run the phishing campaign based on the agreed scenario.
Week 4Share the campaign report, interaction statistics, and recommendations.

The organization covers the cost of the campaign domain. Any user level interaction reporting is discussed during scoping.

External Recon Assessment

Map publicly accessible services and identify opportunities to reduce your digital footprint and external attack surface.

1 month

Deliverable

External Recon Assessment Report with identified exposures, business impact, and recommendations; project plan shared before execution.

Assessment boundary

Includes passive and active reconnaissance of agreed assets. This assessment does not include exploitation.

Week 1

Kickoff, business context, and information request.

Week 2

Passive review of public footprint and available exposure information.

Week 3

Active reconnaissance against agreed public facing assets.

Week 4

Report findings and explain recommendations.

External Red Team Assessment

AI Red Team Assessment

Evaluate in scope AI systems for security and safety failures, including risks such as prompt injection, jailbreaks, sensitive data exposure, and excessive agency.

1 to 2 months

Deliverable

AI Red Team Assessment Report with findings, evidence, business impact, and recommendations; project plan shared before execution.

Approach

Choose a black box, grey box, or white box scope. The assessment references OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications and MITRE ATLAS.

A staging or test environment is recommended to avoid disrupting production systems. Duration depends on scope.
